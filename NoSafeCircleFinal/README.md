# No Safe Circle

A tiny wizard is lost in a dungeon. Something is down there with you. Five doors stand between you and the way out.

Play in the browser — no install needed.

## How to play

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Left-click** the floor | Walk there |
| **Left-click** a door | Walk to it and open it |
| **Right-click** anywhere | Cast a fireball toward the cursor |
| **R** | Play again, after you win or die |

## The rules

**Doors open on a timer.** Click a door and your wizard walks over and works on it. The door swings open, and it stays open only for a moment — once you step through, it slams and locks behind you. There is no going back.

**The enemy hunts you.** It notices you when you get close, and once it has you it follows. Let it reach you and it hits you, over and over. Your health is in the top-left corner.

**Fireballs kill.** One direct hit removes the enemy. Aim with the cursor and right-click. You can fire while running — and running while firing is usually the right idea.

**You can outrun it through a door.** Doors are not just progress, they are an escape. Put one between you and the thing chasing you and it cannot follow.

## Winning and losing

**You escape** by crossing the final door. That is the win.

**You die** when your health reaches zero. The enemy does not need many hits.

Either way, press **R** to start over.

## Tips

- Open the door *before* the enemy is on top of you. The door takes time to open, and standing still next to it is how you die.
- A fireball is cheaper than a fight. You have no melee.
- If you are cornered, run past the enemy rather than away from it — it turns slower than you do.
